## 🛠️ Features

- **Offline Functionality**  
  Use the app without needing an internet connection. Your data stays safe on your device.
  
- **Task Management**  
  Easily add, edit, and delete tasks. Keep track of what you need to do.

- **Calendar Integration**  
  View your tasks in a calendar format. Plan your days more effectively.

- **Countdown Timer**  
  Set countdowns for important tasks or events.

## 🌟 System Requirements

- **Web Browser**  
  This application works with any modern web browser, including Chrome, Firefox, and Safari.

- **Operating System**  
  It is compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux systems.

## 🌍 How to Use

1. **Launching the App**  
   Open the `index.html` file as described above. This will launch the application in your browser.

2. **Creating a Task**  
   Find the 'Add Task' button. Click it to open a form where you can enter your task title and details. Click 'Save' to add it to your list.

3. **Viewing Your Calendar**  
   Switch to the calendar view to see your tasks organized by date. This helps in planning your time.

4. **Using the Countdown Timer**  
   To set a timer, locate the countdown feature and input the desired duration. Click 'Start' to activate it.
